Our Story

❧

Our story may not have followed the conventional path, but it's become something uniquely ours.

We met in July 2025, and before long, we were making memories together—hiking new trails, camping under the stars, golfing, and spending countless evenings around the fire sharing jokes and listening to hours of music.

Some of our favorite moments have been the simplest ones: laughing until we can't breathe, enjoying a quiet evening together, and finding adventure in the everyday.

Along the way, our family grew. Brett brought Makenna into our lives, and together we welcomed our son, Maveryck. We've built a family, shared new adventures, and created a life that is ours.

In July 2026, Brett proposed, and we began looking forward to forever.

Our journey hasn't been conventional, but we wouldn't trade the memories, the laughter, or the life we've built together.

Here's to love, family, adventure, and a lifetime of inside jokes.

What to Wear

❧

Country Chic

We'd love for our guests to join us in a Country Chic look — think dressy, comfortable, and country-inspired with a touch of elegance. It's the perfect blend of rustic charm and polished style for our barn celebration.

Ladies: Flowy midi or maxi dresses, floral dresses, dressy jumpsuits, or skirts with a pretty blouse. Boots, booties, or dressy flats are welcome. A denim jacket, shawl, or cardigan is a lovely layer if the spring evening is cool.

Gentlemen: Button-down shirts (solid, plaid, or subtle print), khakis, dress pants, or nice dark-wash jeans without rips. Vests, blazers, and sport coats are optional. Cowboy boots or clean dress shoes are perfect; ties are optional.

Color inspiration: Forest green, sage, olive, eucalyptus, cream, ivory, warm taupe, tan, and muted florals. Please save white and ivory outfits for the bride.

In short: dressy, comfortable, and country-inspired. Wear something you feel great in!
